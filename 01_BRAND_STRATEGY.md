# Delicious Foods Wah Cantt — Final Brand Strategy

## 1. Brand essence
**Every craving, made easy.**

Delicious Foods brings broad, familiar food choices together in one colorful, convenient local destination.

## 2. Positioning statement
For people in and around Wah Cantt choosing food for themselves, friends, or family, **Delicious Foods Wah Cantt** is the approachable all-round food destination that makes mixed cravings easy to satisfy—from pizza and burgers to pulao, chicken, drinks, and desserts—through a clear menu, shareable deals, and direct ordering.

This is a strategic position, not a claim to be the largest, cheapest, or best.

## 3. Main website message
### Primary recommendation
**Whatever you’re craving, we’ve got it.**

### Interactive/section prompt
**What are you craving?**

### Supporting line
**Pizza, burgers, chicken, pulao, drinks and more—made for good food and great moments in Wah Cantt.**

### Why this works
- Converts breadth into a customer benefit.
- Supports category-led navigation.
- Works for individual and group occasions.
- Is memorable without an unverified superlative.
- Extends naturally to campaigns: “Craving pizza?”, “Craving something crispy?”, “Craving a cool drink?”

## 4. Audience priority
1. Mobile, high-intent local customers ready to order.
2. Families and groups with varied preferences.
3. Deal-seeking students and young adults.
4. Existing customers checking current menu, hours, or location.
5. Local search visitors comparing options.

## 5. Personality
- **Energetic, not chaotic**
- **Friendly, not childish**
- **Confident, not boastful**
- **Affordable, not cheap-looking**
- **Local, not clichéd**
- **Playful, not gimmicky**
- **Modern, not sterile**

## 6. Tone of voice
Use short, concrete, food-first language. Prefer verbs and cravings over abstract claims.

### Do
- “Pick your craving.”
- “Grab a deal.”
- “Call to order.”
- “Pizza for the whole crew.”
- “Find us on Main Shah Wali Road.”
- “Prices shown are being confirmed.” (only in staging/preview where relevant)

### Do not
- “Embark on a culinary journey.”
- “The number-one restaurant in Wah Cantt.”
- “Award-winning excellence.”
- “Authentic since 19XX.”
- Fake urgency, fake scarcity, or invented customer praise.

### Language behavior
Use plain international English with familiar local food terminology. If Urdu is launched, use human-reviewed translation and an Urdu-capable type stack; do not machine-publish operational details without review.

## 7. Messaging pillars
### A. A choice for every craving
Show category breadth through a simple, visual taxonomy—not a long claim list.

### B. Easy to choose, easy to order
Clear pricing, category filters, strong mobile interactions, and direct order channels.

### C. Better together
Use verified deals and group-friendly photography to support family/friend occasions without inventing serving claims.

### D. Proudly local and easy to find
Consistent Wah Cantt address, hours, phone, map, and local context.

### E. Real food, clearly presented
Real restaurant photography, honest availability, and no misleading placeholders.

## 8. Visual principles
1. **Food first:** food occupies the strongest visual focal point.
2. **Cream creates appetite and calm:** default page field is warm cream, not solid purple.
3. **Purple establishes ownership:** header accents, hero field, key bands, selected states.
4. **Yellow signals action/deals:** limited to high-priority actions and merchandising moments.
5. **Orange adds warmth:** secondary emphasis, tags, and illustration details.
6. **Organic, controlled shapes:** one drip/liquid gesture per major composition at most.
7. **Readable playfulness:** display face for short headings; neutral sans for everything functional.
8. **Consistent imagery:** fixed ratios, natural colors, no unrelated stock representation.

## 9. Design principles
- **Craving before catalog:** begin with meaningful choices.
- **Recognition before novelty:** preserve brand cues while cleaning execution.
- **One primary action per context:** avoid CTA competition.
- **Progressive disclosure:** cards scan quickly; detail views hold variants and instructions.
- **Truth in interface:** unavailable, unverified, or future functionality must be explicit.
- **Mobile is the primary storefront:** thumb reach, sticky actions, fast images, short paths.
- **System over one-offs:** all colors, spacing, type, and states come from tokens/components.

## 10. Content principles
- Lead with product/category names customers recognize.
- Descriptions should identify meaningful ingredients or format only when confirmed.
- Keep operational facts centrally sourced.
- Use “Featured favorites” until sales data supports “best sellers.”
- Never embed prices or hours in imagery.
- Indicate stale/unconfirmed data in CMS/admin workflows; avoid public uncertainty labels after owner verification.
- Make corrections easy by separating data from components.

## 11. CTA language system
| Context | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Global | Order now | View menu |
| Hero | Order now | Explore menu |
| Category | View [category] | — |
| Product, Stage 1 | Order this item | View details |
| Product, Stage 2 | Add to order | — |
| Deal | Order deal | View details |
| Contact | Call to order | Get directions |
| Cart | Checkout | Continue browsing |
| Checkout | Place order | Back to cart |
| Confirmation | Track/contact restaurant | Return home |

Use “Add to order” instead of “Add +” where space allows; retain an accessible name for icon-only mobile controls.

## 12. Photography direction
### Subject
Real Delicious Foods dishes, drinks, team/interior, packaging, and storefront. Prioritize hero mixed spread; pizzas; burger/chicken closeups; pulao; drinks with condensation; shareable deals; desserts.

### Art direction
- 3/4 angle and top-down mix.
- Warm, natural light; accurate food color.
- Clean cream or brand-color surfaces with restrained props.
- Consistent plating and packaging.
- Leave negative space for hero copy where planned.
- Photograph variants and deals exactly as sold.

### Technical capture/delivery
- Shoot high-resolution RAW originals.
- Deliver landscape hero, square card, 4:5 social, and detail crops.
- Generate AVIF/WebP derivatives; retain JPEG fallback where needed.
- Avoid text baked into images.
- Record dish name, date, rights, and representation accuracy in asset inventory.

### Placeholder rule
Use labeled neutral placeholders in development. Never imply generated or stock food is an actual Delicious Foods item.

## 13. Brand governance
- Logo is not to be redrawn without approval.
- Provisional colors must be measured against source artwork when files arrive.
- New campaign graphics must use established tokens and type rules.
- Any claim, badge, review, promotion, or delivery promise requires an owner and expiry/review date.
- Quarterly audit: NAP, hours, menu, deals, imagery, social URLs, schema, and order links.
